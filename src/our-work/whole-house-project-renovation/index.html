Project Details

A closer look at the connected main floor, the kitchen, the covered patio, and the bathroom finishes.

Living area and kitchen

Living area and kitchen

The brick fireplace gives the living room a strong focal point. From here, you can see right into the kitchen and across the wood island, which helps the main floor feel connected. I like that the brick, wood, and green cabinetry each have their own character without making the space feel busy.

Kitchen layout

Kitchen layout

The green cabinets give the kitchen some personality, and the wood island adds a warm place to gather. The wide window over the sink brings in daylight and a view of the trees. It is a kitchen that feels useful for everyday cooking as well as time together.

Covered outdoor space

Covered outdoor space

The covered patio extends the living space outdoors. Wood posts, a brick fireplace, and the paved floor give it a solid, comfortable feel. It is easy to picture people sitting here on a cool evening, with a little shelter when Seattle weather changes.

Kitchen details

Kitchen details

This view shows the range wall and the details around it. Green cabinets frame the cooking area, and the light tile keeps the wall clean and simple. The nearby window brings daylight to the prep space, which makes a real difference when you use the kitchen every day.

Bathroom details

Bathroom details

The wood vanity brings the same warm tone into the bathroom that you see elsewhere in the house. A broad mirror helps the room feel open, and the high window adds daylight while keeping the space private. The result is practical, calm, and easy to live with.
