Helpful Answers

Common Renovation Questions

Direct answers about whole-house projects, additions, DADUs, ADUs, basements, focused remodels, permits, project fit, and construction planning.

Is Silver Peak Design & Build a legitimate contractor?+

Yes. Silver Peak Design & Build’s contractor registration can be verified directly through Washington State Labor & Industries. View Silver Peak’s official L&I contractor record.

What does a technical site assessment include?+

A technical site assessment reviews existing conditions, access, structure, utilities, zoning, trees, drainage, side sewer needs, electrical capacity, permits, and project feasibility before design begins.

How do I build a DADU in Seattle?+

Building a DADU in Seattle begins with a site assessment covering zoning, lot access, trees, sewer, utilities, electrical capacity, design options, permits, budget, and construction access.

What is included in kitchen design?+

Kitchen design includes layout, traffic flow, work zones, appliance placement, cabinetry, storage, lighting, islands, materials, ventilation, and build coordination.

What should I plan for bathroom remodeling?+

Bathroom remodeling should address layout, waterproofing, tile, storage, lighting, ventilation, fixtures, accessibility, and durable materials.

Should I build an ADU or DADU?+

An ADU is typically attached to or inside the main home, while a DADU is detached. The right choice depends on lot size, access, zoning, utilities, budget, privacy, and permit feasibility in Seattle.

When should I schedule a site assessment?+

Schedule a technical site assessment after the initial project-fit review and before finalizing design so zoning, access, utilities, structure, trees, sewer, and permit issues can be identified early.

What improves a kitchen remodel?+

Strong kitchen remodeling plans improve layout, storage, lighting, appliance placement, traffic flow, ventilation, material durability, and everyday usability.

Can bathroom remodeling improve accessibility?+

Yes. Bathroom remodeling can include curbless showers, wider clearances, grab bar blocking, slip-resistant materials, better lighting, reachable storage, and comfort-height fixtures.

What utilities affect ADU and DADU projects?+

ADU and DADU planning may require review of electrical capacity, sewer, water service, drainage, heating, ventilation, access, and utility upgrade costs.

Which Seattle neighborhoods do you serve?+

Silver Peak focuses on North Seattle neighborhoods including Greenwood, Phinney Ridge, Ballard, Queen Anne, Wallingford, and Fremont, plus select Eastside communities when the project is a good fit.
