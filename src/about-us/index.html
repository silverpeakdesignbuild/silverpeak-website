Our Story

Built for Bigger Projects.

Silver Peak Design Build is built around one accountable team connecting planning, design, permits, construction, and communication. The goal is a clearer path from the first project conversation through the finished build.

Founder Craig Mauer brings more than 20 years of hands-on construction experience. That field background shapes the way Silver Peak plans projects, communicates with homeowners, and makes practical decisions before and during construction.

Today, Silver Peak leads with whole-house builds and major rebuilds, followed by additions, DADUs, ADUs, and basement remodels. Kitchens and bathrooms remain part of the work, but they are considered after the larger project types and when the scope, schedule, location, and overall fit make sense.

Where We Focus

Projects We Build.

Silver Peak presents its work from biggest to smaller projects: whole-house builds and additions first, then DADUs, ADUs, and basements, followed by kitchens, bathrooms, and select specialty work.

Whole-House Builds & Rebuilds

Large residential projects that may include major structural work, full-property renovation, or rebuilding an existing home into a new residence.

Additions

Attached expansions that add meaningful living space while connecting the new construction to the existing home.

DADUs & ADUs

Detached and attached living spaces planned around site conditions, access, privacy, utilities, zoning, and permit requirements.

Basement Remodels

Substantial lower-level renovations that can create finished living areas, bedrooms, storage, built-ins, or additional dwelling space.

Kitchens & Bathrooms

Select kitchen and bathroom remodels when the project scope, schedule, location, and overall fit align with the team.

Porches, Exteriors & Restoration

Selected exterior improvements, porch work, damage restoration, and detail-focused construction that complements larger residential projects.

Our Process

Project Fit Comes First.

Silver Peak does not schedule a technical site assessment for every inquiry. We first review location, project type, scope, budget direction, and working fit. Qualified projects then move into site assessment, planning, and construction.

01

Project Review & Fit Call

We start with your location, goals, project type, scope, budget range, timeline, and overall working fit. One or more calls may happen before an in-person visit is scheduled.

02

Technical Site Assessment

Qualified projects move to an in-person review of existing conditions, access, structural limits, utilities, site constraints, and overall feasibility.

03

Design & Permits

We connect layout direction, materials, finishes, scope, permit requirements, scheduling, and build strategy so the project is better prepared before construction.

04

Construction

Once scope and planning are defined, construction moves ahead with coordinated communication, quality craftsmanship, and attention to the agreed project plan.

What Is Included

Qualification Before the Site Visit.

Every project starts by confirming that Silver Peak is a good fit for the homeowner and that the project is a good fit for Silver Peak.

  • Project type, location, and scope review
  • Budget range and working-fit discussion
  • One or more fit calls when needed
  • Technical site assessment for qualified projects
  • Design, permits, construction communication, and sequencing

Reviewing location, scope, budget, and fit before an in-person site assessment helps both the homeowner and the Silver Peak team use their time well and move forward with clearer expectations.

Core Service Area

North Seattle & Eastside, North of I-90.

Silver Peak focuses on North Seattle and Eastside communities north of I-90. Within that broader area, we prefer northern routes and communities where site visits, construction oversight, and communication can stay practical.

Primary Coverage

North Seattle and the Northern Eastside.

Our normal market is north of I-90, with the strongest focus on North Seattle and northern Eastside communities. When possible, we prefer projects north of SR 520 so travel, site visits, and construction oversight stay practical.

Our primary northern focus includes Bothell, Mill Creek, and Woodinville, along with North Seattle. We also consider select projects in Redmond and Kirkland when the location, scope, budget, timing, and working fit make sense.

Outside our normal service area: Projects south of I-90 are generally outside Silver Peak’s coverage area.
